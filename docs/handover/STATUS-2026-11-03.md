## #171 review points (high effort)

2. The star under the previous mouse point is re-reached on every move, resetting its wait: skip it, as C#'s HitPath starts at i = 1.
3. Pending entries keyed by path: an old read's `dropped("A")` wipes a newer request for A. Key by ask id.
4. Steps run while no frames are drawn pile onto the next frame: report them as "between frames".
5. Smooth `[Frames]` lines every 10 s while a job bar animates: only write them when galaxy steps ran.
6. `[Sweep]` windows are clock time: start at the first sound of a stretch, write the rest on shutdown.
7. Late answers blamed on the step at the first 25 ms look: blame the step seen most.
8. Pruning past 64 entries drops the waiting star: keep ones reached in the last 2 s.
9. Pass the page into `late()`.
