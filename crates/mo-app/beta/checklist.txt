# The beta checklist on Settings > Developer (mo_app::beta). Each step is
#   name | what to do | what you should see
# under an "== Area" heading. A step's name is how its mark is kept in beta-checklist.json: never change or reuse one
# (reword the step instead; a step that's gone keeps its old mark in the file). A thread that builds something that
# needs trying in real use adds a step here. The Self-check steps (auto.*) are marked by the app itself, from what
# mo_app::self_check finds. Live tests with a DAW only use a project called
# "MO Live Test..." (rule 10).

== Start
start.release | Start the app from the launcher's Release shortcut, then close it and start it again | It opens in a few seconds both times; Settings > Developer says Build: Release
start.no-old-dbs | Start the app with the old library.db, sounds.db and midi.db gone | No error card; the Songs, Sounds and MIDI pages all fill
start.close-clean | Use the app for a while, then close it with the window's X | It closes at once; no new crash-....log in the logs folder

== Songs
songs.list | Open the Songs page, search for a song, pick a few chips | The list fills at once and the search and chips narrow it
songs.open-each-daw | Open one FL song, one Bitwig song and one Studio One song from the Songs card | Each opens in its own DAW
songs.preview | Pick ten songs of different ages, from all three DAWs | Each song's arrangement preview shows its clips, with bars of the right length
songs.preview-old | Pick a Bitwig song made before Bitwig 6, and a Studio One song with takes or layers | The preview isn't empty
songs.time-signature | Pick a song in 3/4 or 6/8 if you have one | The bars in the preview are the right length
songs.fix | On a song with missing samples, use Fix, then open the "(fixed)" copy in its DAW | The original is untouched; the fixed copy opens and plays with nothing missing
songs.to-bitwig | On an FL song, use Open in Bitwig | A Bitwig project opens with the same tracks, clips and tempo
songs.to-bitwig-automation | In that converted song, look at tempo, volume, pan and on/off automation | It moves the same way as in FL

== Sounds
sounds.browse | Open Sounds, scroll fast, pick and play sounds, resize the window | Smooth scrolling, waveforms draw, each sound plays at once
sounds.filters | Use the type, key and year filters and the search together | The list narrows the way the chips say
sounds.year-strip | Switch the year strip between Made, Changed, Opened and First used | The years look believable for your sounds
sounds.hide-duplicates | Switch Hide duplicates on and off | Only real copies of a sound hide; nothing else goes
sounds.like-favourites | Move the Sounds like my favourites slider | Similar sounds show within a second or two, and they sound alike
sounds.bitwig-favourites | Open the Favourites from Bitwig card | Most of your Bitwig favourites are found; Save as sounds puts them in "From Bitwig"
sounds.drag-bitwig | Drag a sound from the app onto a Bitwig track | The sound lands on the track and plays
sounds.drag-fl | Drag a sound from the app into FL's playlist or a channel | The sound lands and plays
sounds.trim-silence | Drag a sound with Trim silence on | A trimmed copy is made in your export folder (not on C:), and that's what lands in the DAW
sounds.send-bitwig | Send a sound, and a kit onto Drum Machine pads, to Bitwig | They arrive on the right track and pads
sounds.galaxy | Open the Galaxy: zoom, pan, use the arrow keys, sweep over stars | 60 frames a second, sounds play as you sweep, grey dust for filtered-out stars isn't too faint

== MIDI
midi.page | Open the MIDI page and pick a few MIDI files | It opens without a stall; the piano roll draws at once
midi.play | Play a part, change the speed with the BPM stepper and switch the Metronome on | It plays in time and the changes are heard straight away
midi.play-fl | Play a part into FL Studio over the MIDI port | The selected FL channel plays it
midi.fits | On one of your songs, open Fits my song | A list of parts that fit comes up within a few seconds
midi.fits-play | Play with the song in the app, and try Octave up and down | It sounds in key and in time with the song
midi.fits-by-hand | Pick the song's parts and bars by hand, close and reopen the song | Your pick is remembered
midi.fits-bitwig | In an "MO Live Test" Bitwig project, use Play in Bitwig | Bitwig loops the song's bars with the part playing
midi.fits-put-in-song | In an "MO Live Test" Bitwig project, use Put in the song, then Undo in Bitwig | The part is recorded at the right bars; Undo takes it out
midi.bar-line | Drag a MIDI part into Bitwig and FL and loop it | It loops cleanly with no extra bar at the end

== Presets
presets.list | Open Presets and use its filters | Synths only lists instruments, not a song's effects
presets.drag-keyscape | Drag Keyscape and another synth preset from a Bitwig song, and from a preset file | An instrument track with the right sound, not the default and no error
presets.no-template | Send a preset that Bitwig never saved (Hive, say) to Bitwig | It loads with its sound
presets.audition | Audition a preset in Bitwig, then Stop, then Keep | It plays on a new track; Stop never moves Bitwig's play start; Keep leaves the track

== Plugins
plugins.list | Open Plugins and look through your plugins | Every plugin you have is listed, with its size
plugins.move | Move one plugin you can spare to another drive | It still loads in your DAW (a link is left behind)
plugins.clean | Try Clean up or Uninstall on one plugin you don't use | Only that plugin goes, and the DAW still opens

== Space
space.look | Open the Space page | The cards fill and the sizes look right
space.remove-put-back | Remove one small thing (an old autosave or a spare copy), then Put back | It goes to the Recycle Bin, and Put back brings it back where it was
space.old-copies | Look at the "(old copy)" reminder | It shows with its size
space.fixed-to-bin | Use "Send every fixed Bitwig project to the Recycle Bin" | Only "(fixed)" projects go, to the Recycle Bin

== Backup
backup.first | Make a first backup of a few songs to a spare drive | It finishes and the songs open from the backup
backup.older | Change a song, back up again, and open Older versions | The old version is kept and can be removed
backup.gone | Delete a test file from your music, back up again | The backup keeps it, with a warning

== Curves
curves.import | Open Curves | Your Serum, Vital, LFOTool and Duck shapes are all there and look right
curves.edit | Edit a curve: paint, Randomise, join two, quantise, then save | Each edit does what it says; Undo works; the save is kept
curves.drag | Drag a curve into Bitwig, and Hear it on a tone | Bitwig takes it as a curve; it sounds like it looks

== Settings
settings.data-folder | Look at Settings > Developer's data and logs folders | Both are on I:, and new reports land there, not on C:
settings.helper | Switch on Keep the helper running, sign out and back in | The helper starts once; clicking its icon doesn't open a second app
settings.keys | Change a shortcut on the Shortcuts tab, use it, then Default | The new key works everywhere and Default puts it back
settings.f12 | Press F12, write a note with a picture, close the app halfway, reopen and press F12 | Your draft is still there; the saved note is in the notes folder

== Bitwig
bitwig.install | Install the Bitwig extension from Settings > DAWs | DAWs says it's connected, version 3.29
bitwig.watch | Play a Bitwig song with the app open | The app follows the tracks, selection and play position without lag
bitwig.sampler | Use Try in Sampler on a sound | The sound goes in Bitwig's Sampler on the right device, and its name is right
bitwig.stop | Press Stop in the app while Bitwig is stopped | Bitwig's play start doesn't jump to bar 1

== FL Studio
fl.install | Install the FL script from Settings > DAWs | DAWs says it's installed
fl.live-test | In an empty "MO Live Test automation" FL project, run the script's measure | It finishes; Claude reads the saved project afterwards

== Test plugin
plugin.install | Install the latest test plugin (DAWs closed), open it in Bitwig and in FL | It opens in both, with every tab
plugin.galaxy | Use the plugin's Galaxy: mini map, arrows, trail, drag a sound out | Smooth, and the dragged sound lands on a track
plugin.presets | Look for the plugin's presets in Bitwig's browser | Your favourites and preset files are listed once each and load
plugin.knobs | Map the plugin's knobs to a remote page and use In time | The knobs move what they say, in time with the song
plugin.listen | Send a track into the plugin's Track in | It names the notes it hears
plugin.record | Record a take, then close the DAW straight away | The take is kept
plugin.chord-check | Play chords into the Chord check tab and label a few takes | The takes are kept in the data folder's Chord check
plugin.unload | In FL, remove the plugin from a channel, then close the project | FL doesn't crash

== Files
files.real-check | Run "Real files check.cmd" from the tools folder | It ends with no panics; Claude reads the summary

== Self-check
# Marked by the app itself (mo_app::self_check), once a day and from Run the self-check; never by hand.
auto.crashes | The app looks for crash logs from the last 7 days | None
auto.freezes | The app looks for freezes of a second or more in its logs | None
auto.errors | The app looks for error lines in its logs | None
auto.start | The app reads how long each start took to the window | Usually 5 s or less
auto.data-folder | The app looks at where its data folder is | Not on C:
